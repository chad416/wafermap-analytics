# Dataset source and terms

This repository does **not** contain the WM-811K dataset. `scripts/fetch_dataset.py` downloads
it to `data/raw/` (git-ignored) and checks its SHA-256.

- **Dataset:** WM-811K (`LSWMD.pkl`, 2,095,505,977 bytes), 811,457 wafer maps from real fab
  production, 172,950 of them labelled with one of nine failure-pattern classes.
- **Original publisher:** MIR Lab, National Taiwan University. The original download page on
  mirlab.org no longer resolves (checked 2026-10-29).
- **Citation (please cite when using the data):**
  M.-J. Wu, J.-S. R. Jang and J.-L. Chen, "Wafer Map Failure Pattern Recognition and Similarity
  Ranking for Large-Scale Data Sets," *IEEE Transactions on Semiconductor Manufacturing*, vol. 28,
  no. 1, pp. 1-12, Feb. 2015.
- **Mirror used by the fetch script:**
  <https://huggingface.co/datasets/lslattery/wafer-defect-detection> (ungated). Its dataset card
  cites the paper above and states no licence. The file's SHA-256 is pinned in the script and in
  `backend/src/wafermap/data/prepare.py`.
- **Other copy:** the Kaggle dataset "WM-811K wafer map" (`qingyi/wm811k-wafer-map`) has the same
  file size and is tagged "CC0: Public Domain" by its uploader. The uploader is not the original
  publisher, so that tag is a third party's declaration, not a grant from MIR Lab.
- **How it is used here:** non-commercial research and portfolio work, with attribution. The raw
  data is not redistributed. Before any commercial use, clear the terms with the original
  publisher.

Committed artefacts derived from the data are aggregate statistics, plots, five synthetic sample
maps (`frontend/public/samples/`, generated by `scripts/make_sample_maps.py`, not taken from the
dataset) and the two trained models in `models/`. The screenshots in `docs/screenshots/` and
`reports/figures/class_examples.png` show a few individual wafer maps from the dataset as
images. No per-wafer data files are committed.
